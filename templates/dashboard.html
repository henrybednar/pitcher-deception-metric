Deception+

Deception+ measures how far a pitcher's results land above or below what a model predicts from each pitch's speed, spin, movement and location, plus the batter, catcher, count and park.

The short version

What it is

A model predicts what should happen on every pitch. Deception+ adds up how far a pitcher beats those predictions in {{N_COMPOSITE}} areas (whiff, chase, weak contact, swing timing, whiff miss distance), giving steadier areas more weight.

How to read a number

100 is average and each 10 points is one step, so 120 is two steps up. Every score has a 95% interval on the leaderboard. Check it before ranking two close pitchers.

What it does not prove

The score is whatever the model cannot explain, so calling it deception is a hypothesis. It tracks Stuff+ (r={{STUFF_R}}). Sequencing, tunneling, spin mirroring, tempo and arm slot each explain at most {{DRIVER_R2_MAX_PCT}} of the differences between pitchers.

The stats in plain English

r (correlation)
How closely two numbers move together: 0 is no link, 1 is a perfect match. Deception+ and Stuff+ sit at {{STUFF_R}}, a loose link.Pearson r.
Reliability
Odd and even games scored separately, then compared. High means skill, low means luck.Split-half r, Spearman-Brown corrected.
AUC
Given a pitch that drew a whiff and one that did not, how often the model rates the whiff higher. 50% is a coin flip.Area under the ROC curve, out of fold.
R²
The share of differences a model explains. 0.23 means 23%.Cross-validated, grouped by pitcher.
Calibration slope
Do predicted rates match real ones? At 1.0, a predicted 30% happens about 30% of the time.Actual on predicted rate, by decile.
Forecast gain (ΔR²)
How much more of next season a piece explains once added. Timing's {{DELTA_TIMING}} is {{DELTA_TIMING_PCT}} points more.Change in cross-validated R².
p
How often a gain this big would appear by luck if the piece added nothing. Under 0.01 passes here.Added-term test, errors clustered on pitcher.
95% interval
The range likely to hold the true value. A gain whose range stays above zero, or a score whose range clears 100, is clear.Scores: the wider of bootstrap and analytic.

Does it just track Stuff+?

Partly. The two correlate at r={{STUFF_R}} (95% interval {{STUFF_R_CI}}), so better stuff tends to score higher, but Stuff+ explains only about {{STUFF_R2_PCT}} of the differences in Deception+.

The model already accounts for each pitch's measured speed, spin, movement, release and location. Stuff+ knows more than those measurements, so some of it still shows up. The model never uses Stuff+.

Does it hold up?

Mostly. Each piece is checked two ways: is it steady, and does it help forecast next season? The steadiest is {{MOST_LABEL}} ({{MOST_R}}) and the noisiest is {{LEAST_LABEL}} ({{LEAST_R}}).

Before you quote a score

  • Volume matters. Year to year, r is {{YOY_QUAL_R}} when the pitcher qualified in both seasons, against {{YOY_R}} overall.
  • Relievers average {{RP_MEAN}} against {{SP_MEAN}} for starters even after adjusting for fatigue, so part of the gap may be role. The leaderboard filters by role.
  • Left-handers average {{LHP_GAP}} points against right-handers ({{LHP_N}} against {{RHP_N}} pitcher-seasons, p={{LHP_P}}). The gap is either real or leftover model error, and these data cannot say which.
  • Three seasons (2024 to 2026) means no aging curves and only two season-to-season pairs.

Every known limit and the audit log are on the methodology page.
