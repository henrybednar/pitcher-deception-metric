Each index is the pitcher's actual rate minus the rate a model expects from the pitch (velocity, spin, movement, location), the batter, the catcher, the count, the park, and pitcher-batter handedness. Small samples are shrunk toward the average. 100 is the average scored pitcher-season for each index and the average qualified pitcher-season for Deception+, and 10 points is one standard deviation. Deception+ is a reliability-weighted average of 5 components: whiff, chase, weak contact, timing and whiff miss distance.

Timing is the gap between where the bat met the ball and the hitter's estimated ideal contact depth, so early and late contact both count. It is scored on swings that made contact. Whiff miss distance is Statcast's distance between ball and barrel at closest approach on swings and misses. *Ground ball is scored and shown but is not part of Deception+, because it is a different skill from the other components (its index correlates at most 0.191 with them). Timing dir is the signed timing gap in inches against a stuff-and-location baseline: positive means contact farther out front than the hitter's ideal (earlier) and negative means deeper (later). It is not shaded because it is not on the 100 scale.

Role comes from each pitcher's real share of appearances that were starts: 80% or more is a starter (SP), under 10% is a reliever (RP), and anything between is mixed (MR). Where that isn't available, role falls back to a proxy from median pitches per appearance: 60 or more is a starter, under 30 is a reliever.

Deception+ has a split-half reliability of r=0.743 and a year-over-year correlation of r=0.600. Among its members whiff miss distance is the most reliable (r=0.644) and weak contact the least (r=0.271). Called strike was removed from the score and this table because it is stable within a season but does not carry over between seasons (year-over-year r=0.153). The dashboard has its numbers.

Pitch data comes from Baseball Savant (Statcast). Stuff+ comes from FanGraphs and was entered by hand. This project is independent and not affiliated with MLB, Baseball Savant, or FanGraphs. The dashboard has the full method, the validation table, and the audit log.
